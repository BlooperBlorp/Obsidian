Aka Rudolph van Richten

Real story (from his mouth):
I come from a land called Darkon. There I married my childhood sweetheart, Ingrid, and together we had a son, Erasmus. When he was fourteen, Erasmus was stolen away by Vistani and sold to a vampire named Baron Metus to be used as a companion. By the time I found my son, it was too late: the baron had already transformed Erasmus into a vampire spawn. Erasmus begged me to end his suffering, which I did by pounding a wooden stake through his chest. Baron Metus avenged that deed by killing Ingrid, and van Richten has lived with the horror of his family's destruction ever since. After destroying Baron Metus in turn, van Richten sought revenge against the Vistani and took up a life of hunting evil monsters.

I'm not a young man anymore. He knows his road is coming to an end, but my work isn't done. I have come to Barovia to kill [Strahd von Zarovich](https://5e.tools/bestiary.html#strahd%20von%20zarovich_cos), the greatest vampire of them all. I have studied Strahd for years and know I can't hope to best the vampire in a straight-up confrontation: I must wait for the right moment to strike. 

I have evidence to suggest that Strahd periodically hibernates in his coffin, sometimes for years, when all is quiet in the realm. 

He is trying to learn more about the Keepers of the Feather—a society of wereravens that oppose Strahd—while trying not to expose the secret society to their mutual enemy. He thinks the wereravens might prove helpful when the time comes. I also wants to take out as many of Strahd's spies as he can, starting with evil Vistani.
