### Important links between pre-strahd and post-strahd
- The link itself:
	- Within his study (somehow), Strahd learned about a weakpoint in the weave and figured it may have been a way to escape from Barovia. Not that he like particularly wants to leave, thats not a huge motivator of his, but hes the kinda guy to fuck with stuff for the sake of it 
		- He has been poking and prodding at this point in the weave, to see if he could get anything useful out of it somehow, and that created the [[gash in the weave]]. The gash in the weave is actually a non-planar but arcane portal between Barovia, the land of Strahd, and the sword coast. This means one cannot traverse it physically but the weave can penetrate between the two locations. 
			- Thus, **all individuals touched by the gash in the weave in one way or another were actually touched by Strahd's powerful magic gifted to him by the dark powers, marking them with an arcane trace from Barovia"**
		- Seperately, [[Efarius Covenmane]] was this douchebag wizard who heard about the gash in the weave and was like "wait holdup some all powerful dude must be involved with this, I have to link up with this guy", so he went to check it out.
			- Being pressured by the party, Efarius decided to just say fuck it and channeled powerful Conjuration magic to summon whoever was meddling with the weave to him. He was able to do this as through his research at the gash in the weave he was able to find a strong arcane fingerprint left behind from whoever was meddling with the weave, he targeted this arcane fingerprint.
	- This all comes to a head in the players fateful encounter with Efarius, as he attempts to (unknowingly) pull Barovia into Faerun. As the connection between Barovia and Faerun is destabilized following his defeat, the dark powers step in to intervene, slamming the gash shut, and pulling all of the pieces of Barovia back there.
- The players, now finding themselves accidentally pulled into a foreign land, need to reckon with the consequences of that. 
	- The players will have a particular interest is figuring out what the hell happened to all of the other individuals possibly pulled with them.
