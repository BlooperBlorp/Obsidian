### Mists of Ravenloft
- A creature who starts it's turn (or enters) the fog must succeed a DC 20 Con save or gain a level of exhaustion 
- No matter *anything* at all, a creature will get turned around in the fog and find itself back in Barovia.

- The holy symbol of ravenkind is under marina's monument in Brerez
